# 2026-10-27: search with a real embedding, at no cost

Runs of the question check's search alone (`--search-only`), over the saved replies of the
[first DeepSeek run](2026-10-27-deepseek.md), to find where that run lost its answers. No
paid model was called. A Chinese copy is in [2026-10-27-search.zh.md](2026-10-27-search.zh.md).

## Conclusions

- **The first run lost its answers before the model saw them.** Of the 135 questions with an
  answer, the model was given text that states it for 64. The engine answered 58 of those
  right and none of the other 71. Long context answered 131 of the 135 right.
- **Three things cut what the model was given.** Two are stand-ins; the third is a trade-off
  of our own.
  - **Stand-in vectors.** The vectors were hashed words, so a Chinese or Japanese question
    could not find the English expense policy. Search found 1 of the policy's 48 such
    questions.
  - **Stand-in reranker.** It reorders by shared words, so passages that only share the
    question's words rank above the passage that answers it.
  - **Snippets.** Each passage reached the model as a snippet of about 80 tokens, picked by
    the words it shares with the question. Across languages, or when the question says it
    differently, the snippet leaves the answer out.
- **With a real embedding, no stand-in reranker and whole passages, the answer is in what
  the model reads for 116 of the 135 questions.**
  - Reading the search's top 3 documents whole, it is there for 125; with the top 5, for 129.
  - The reference row reproduces the paid run's 64 exactly, so the rows compare.
- **What changes.**
  - The engine now gives an answer each passage whole, while a search hit still shows its
    snippet.
  - `config/gateway.deepseek.yaml` uses the local embedding and no reranker.
  - The next paid run answers the 150 questions again on that setup. It costs about CNY 2,
    since reading comes from the saved replies.
- **Open.**
  - Whether the model answers right when given whole passages: the paid run says.
  - A real reranker that scores whole passages.
  - Reading the top documents whole, for the questions whose passages still miss.

## Where the first run lost its 69 answers

The gap between the engine (71 right of 150) and long context (140), by what the questions
ask:

| Questions | Count | Engine | Plain retrieval | Long context | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expense policy, asked in Chinese or Japanese (the policy is English only) | 52 | 4 | 3 | 48 | 44 |
| Shinkansen, long articles in each language | 39 | 17 | 14 | 35 | 18 |
| Expense policy, asked in English | 26 | 20 | 17 | 24 | 4 |
| Kubernetes pages | 24 | 21 | 17 | 24 | 3 |
| Nothing in the drive answers | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 0 |

The 4 of the first row are questions nothing in force answers. Where the model was given the
answer, the engine beat plain retrieval, 58 against 45 of 64. In English, 2 of the engine's
misses on the policy are values from the 2023 copy on the shared drive, which normalization
does not pair with the handbook's policy as an older edition.

- "As given today" is what `found` checks: a statement, or a passage's snippet.
- "Whole passages" reads the same 8 passages and 8 statements whole.
- "Top documents" takes the distinct documents of one wide search's hits, in order, and reads
  them whole.
- Without a reranker, the order is the fusion of the vector and keyword indexes.

## What ran

- **Engine.** The engine of the first run (`main` at dc019fa), with this change's
  `--search-only` check. Answers still read snippets, which is what "as given today" counts.
- **Embedding.** Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B in 8-bit ONNX (`onnx-community/Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B-ONNX`,
  `q8`), served by `infra/local-embeddings` on the container's 4 CPUs. Its query instruction
  is "Given a question, retrieve passages that answer it". The drive took about 27 minutes
  to embed.
- **Config.** `config/gateway.local-embeddings.yaml`, once with the stand-in reranker added,
  and once with the stand-in embedding in place of the real one for the reference row.
- **Commands,** each setting on a fresh stack:

  ```bash
  make clean up migrate
  pnpm bench cache import replies.jsonl --stack --gateway config/gateway.local-embeddings.yaml
  DEEPSEEK_SEARCH_ONLY_KEY=none pnpm bench ingest examples/company-drive --today 2026-10-27 \
    --gateway config/gateway.local-embeddings.yaml --stack --search-only --json search.json
  ```

- **A fresh stack each time.** The bench's permission graph is kept in memory. A second run
  on the same database finds the documents unchanged, so it registers no permissions and
  search reads nothing.
- **Cost: none.**
  - All 339 extraction calls and 42 chunk-header calls came from the saved replies.
  - The stand-in named the topics (14 calls).
  - Embeddings ran locally.
