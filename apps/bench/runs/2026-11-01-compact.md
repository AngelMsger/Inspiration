# 2026-11-01: a compact answer, a mature RAG and a fast model, weighed on cost, accuracy and latency

The round the product owner asked for on 2026-11-01: judge Inspiration on cost, accuracy and
latency together, on the cheapest provider, with a cheap model from another provider only to
back up a conclusion. On the company drive's 150 questions it ran a free search check, then
DeepSeek (a 10-question gate and the full set), then GPT-6 Luna on the same sources, then a
free evidence audit on the real reads. A Chinese copy is in
[2026-11-01-compact.zh.md](2026-11-01-compact.zh.md).

## Conclusions

- **A mature RAG answers a few more questions right; Inspiration reads far less, and its
  compact answer is also faster.** On DeepSeek, of 150: the mature RAG 137, Inspiration 134,
  the compact answer 128. The compact answer reads 2,075 input tokens on average, a fifth of
  the mature RAG's 9,936, costs a quarter as much, and its model answers in a median 1.32
  seconds against 1.66. Inspiration's standard answer reads 5,538 tokens at 0.0060 CNY against
  0.0104, but its model takes a median 1.83 seconds, a little longer than the mature RAG's.
- **Every answer Inspiration loses to the mature RAG has one of two causes, both known.**
  - The 2023 copy of the policy on the shared drive: normalization does not pair it with the
    renamed handbook policy as an older edition, so its $2,500 marketing limit answers as if
    current. 3 questions for Inspiration, 4 for the compact answer (with its $80 internet
    cap). This has been on the list since the [second run](2026-10-27-whole.md).
  - The compact answer's two passages: the Tokaido line's yearly ridership is in the English
    article only, no statement holds it, and two passages from the Japanese article crowd it
    out. 4 questions. One Japanese hotel question also got "not stated". The free search check
    with the saved translations confirms both: the compact input lacks the answer for exactly
    these 5 questions. It holds the answer for 126 of the 135 with one, Inspiration's for 131, the
    mature RAG's for 131.
  - Inspiration answers no question the mature RAG misses. On this tidy drive, governance
    adds no right answer over the rule "prefer the governed source, then the newest file", as
    [the decisive test](../../../docs/design/validation.md#the-decisive-test) predicted; only
    stale and conflicting cases can show what it adds.
  - Neither cause has a free fix; see [Checked after the run](#checked-after-the-run).
- **On a cheaper, faster model, Inspiration ties the mature RAG.** GPT-6 Luna, at US$0.10 per
  million input tokens: Inspiration answers 134 of 150, as many as the mature RAG, and the compact answer 129. The weaker model costs the mature RAG 3 answers on the long Shinkansen articles. Inspiration gains 3 it had answered on DeepSeek with a neighbouring allowance: an ultrawide monitor's cap, which no document in use states, and which the mature RAG answers from an old copy. Luna's answering time barely depends on the input: a median 1.07 seconds for the compact answer, 1.16 for Inspiration, 1.18 for the mature RAG. An answer costs US$0.00024, 0.00059 and 0.00104.
- **Search takes most of the time on this machine.** The search before an answer took a
  median 11.4 seconds: 9.1 the CPU reranker, 1.1 translating the question. Both RAG paths pay
  it and long context pays none. A product reranks on a GPU, so these seconds say nothing
  about its latency; only the answering model's time compares across paths.
- **The audit on the real reads finds no right value lost.** What governance gives the model
  keeps the right value and its document for all 135 keyed questions, drops none the raw
  documents state, and cites no document beyond what the reader may read; the 15 "not stated"
  questions have no value to audit. It ran in the search-only check, at no cost. It checks that
  the right value survives, not that an old one is marked: the 2023 copy's $2,500 still reaches
  the model as current.
- **Cost: CNY 2.95 on DeepSeek** (balance CNY 101.56 before, 98.61 after) **and US$0.28
  on OpenAI**, metered at OpenAI's published prices, since its balance cannot be read with an
  API key. Leaving the long-context paths out of the full run saved about CNY 4; the gate
  already gives their cost and latency.

- **Models.** `deepseek-flash` with thinking off for everything on DeepSeek. `gpt-6-luna` with

  `answer_question@3`, `translate_question@1`, and the reading prompts of the earlier runs,

  response cache:

## Checked after the run

On 2026-11-01, after this log was first reported, both causes were checked at no cost, and
neither fix was made.

- **Pairing the renamed copy by text is not safe.** A rule that accepts a candidate under
  another title within 9 bits and at least 20% shared text pairs the 2023 copy with the 2024
  backup on the drive, and nothing else there, in the demo corpus, the control or Kubernetes.
  On a synthetic corpus of 1,500 documents it also pairs 35 different policies written from one
  template, which share up to half their text (the account security and lost device policies of
  one department), and retrieval would hide a governed policy behind its sibling.
- **The marketing loss is a search loss.** The current policy states "under $5,000" at the end of
  a 1,822-character passage about laptops. For the English question it is in none of the first 16
  passages of any path; the 2023 and 2022 copies' table rows rank first. The mature RAG answered
  3 of the 6 right only by reaching the 2024 backup's copy of the laptop passage, which
  Inspiration reads as replaced by the current policy.
- **Two passages from two documents gain nothing.** With the compact answer's passages taken from
  different documents, its input holds the answer for 126 of 135, as before (2 gained, 2 lost).
  The Tokaido ridership, 159 million, is stated only by the 2024 edition of the English article;
  the current one no longer says it.

So rerunning the answers would change nothing; the spec's next step is the two topics' cases.
