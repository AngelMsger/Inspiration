# 2026-10-27: answers read whole passages, with a real embedding

The second paid run on DeepSeek: the company drive's 150 questions answered again, after the
[free search check](2026-10-27-search.md) found where the [first run](2026-10-27-deepseek.md)
lost its answers. Reading came from the first run's saved replies, so only answers were paid
for. A Chinese copy is in [2026-10-27-whole.zh.md](2026-10-27-whole.zh.md).

## Conclusions

- **Inspiration now answers 116 of 150 right, plain RAG 109 and long context 140.** The first
  run gave 71, 60 and 140. Three things changed, none of them the model or the reading:
  - a real multilingual embedding, in place of hashed words;
  - no stand-in reranker;
  - each passage reaches the answer whole, not as an 80-token snippet.
- **The gap to long context shrank from 69 questions to 24.** Long context gets 25 questions
  Inspiration misses, and Inspiration 1 that long context misses. Of the 25:
  - 14 are search: the 8 passages and 8 statements still lack the answer. Eight are the
    English-only policy asked in Chinese or Japanese. Six are long Shinkansen articles, such
    as the network's total length, which only one sentence states.
  - 4 are stale values from the 2023 copy of the policy on the shared drive. Normalization
    does not pair it with the handbook's policy as an older edition, because the policy has
    since been renamed, from Expenses to Global Travel and Expense Policy.
  - 4 are answers the key marked wrong because of its own gaps (see the next point).
  - 3 are the model: given the passage, it answered a different limit or said nothing.
- **The answer key had two gaps, now corrected.** The Japanese article dates the Tōkaidō
  opening "1964年（昭和39年）10月1日", which the key did not accept as 1 October 1964. The
  Chinese article states the Chūō opening as 2034, but the key listed only the English one,
  so three answers citing it were marked wrong. (The Japanese article says 2035 or later; one
  of the three gives both.) Rescored with the corrected key, for nothing, Inspiration answers
  120 of 150, plain RAG 112 and long context 140, with the same stale answers. The run's own
  numbers stand as scored; the corrections are recorded in
  [examples/company-drive](../../../examples/company-drive/README.md#corrections).
- **Against plain RAG, governance now adds 7 questions and 4 fewer stale answers.**
  - Changed facts: 44 against 40 of 66, 6 points, short of the 20 points the bar asks.
  - Stale answers: 6 against 10 (4% of 150, within the bar of 5%).
  - Facts no document in use states: 10 of 15 each. With whole passages the model reads
    more of the policy and answers 3 of the monitor questions with a neighbouring allowance,
    where the first run said "not stated".
- **An answer reads 1.7% of what long context reads.** Inspiration's answers average about
  4,960 input tokens, long context's 285,000. Off-peak that is about CNY 0.005 per answer,
  against CNY 0.29 for long context without DeepSeek's prefix cache. With the cache, one
  reader asking about this one small corpus pays about CNY 0.006 for long context too, and
  reading the drive once cost Inspiration CNY 2.37, as much as about 300 long-context
  questions. So the cost advantage holds where the cache cannot: a corpus larger than the
  window, readers with different permissions, and documents that change between questions.
- **Cost: CNY 1.46** for the 10-question gate and the 150 questions (balance CNY 103.02
  before, 101.56 after).

## What ran

- **Engine.** PR #59 at 23f41d5: answers read whole passages; `config/gateway.deepseek.yaml`
  embeds with Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (8-bit ONNX, served locally by `infra/local-embeddings`)
  and has no reranker. The run thread ran it, off-peak, 17:19 to 17:33 UTC.
- **Model.** `deepseek-flash`, thinking off, as in the first run.
- **Commands.**

  ```bash
  make clean up migrate
  pnpm bench cache import replies-with-qwen3-embeddings.jsonl --stack --gateway config/gateway.deepseek.yaml
  pnpm bench ingest examples/company-drive --today 2026-10-27 --context 1000000 \
    --gateway config/gateway.deepseek.yaml --stack --questions 10 --json gate10.json
  # then, on a fresh stack with the replies exported after the gate, the same without --questions
  ```

- **What it paid for.**

  | Run | Paid calls | Input tokens | From DeepSeek's cache | Output tokens |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Gate, 10 questions | 27 (20 answers, 7 topic names) | 106,296 | 7,424 | 2,611 |
  | 150 questions | 280 answers | 1,388,118 | 155,648 | 27,434 |

  Everything else came from the saved replies: 339 extraction calls, 42 chunk headers, 501
  embeddings, the topic names and long context's 150 answers.
- **Rescore.** `rescore.ts` applies the bench's scoring rule to the saved answers of
  `real150-whole.json` under a given key, without calling a model. Under the key the run used
  it reproduces all 450 outcomes (150 questions, three ways); under the corrected key 7
  change, all to right.
