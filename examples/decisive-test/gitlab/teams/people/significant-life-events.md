---
title: "Significant life events: gifts and flowers"
---

When a team member, or someone in their immediate family, has a significant life event (a
birth, an adoption, a wedding, a bereavement or a serious illness), their manager or a
teammate may send a gift or flowers on behalf of the team. People Operations owns this
practice.

## How much

Gifts and flowers for a significant life event are capped at $100 USD per event, including
shipping and tax. Cash and gift cards are not allowed.

## How

1. Order the gift yourself and expense it in Navan under "Team Member Gifts", naming the event
   in the description.
2. The cost is charged to the sender's departmental budget.
3. For a bereavement, check with the team member's manager first, so the family is not sent
   more than one gift.
