---
title: "New hire FAQ: expenses and your home office"
---

Answers to the expense questions new team members ask in their first weeks. People
Operations keeps this page; the [Global Travel and Expense Policy](/handbook/finance/expenses/)
is the policy.

## How do I get reimbursed?

Submit expenses in Navan with a receipt. You get access to Navan on your first day; if you do
not, ask in #expense-reporting-inquiries.

## What can I spend on my home office?

You get a home office budget of $1,500 per year for your desk, chair, monitor and other
equipment from the approved list. Buy what you need with your own card and expense it in Navan.

## Can I use a co-working space?

Yes, if working from home is not practical. There is no set limit for co-working space fees
per month. We ask that you choose an economic option that suits your needs and is a reasonable
cost for your location, and get your manager's approval first.

## I am travelling for onboarding. What is covered?

- Your flight, train and hotel, booked in Navan.
- Internet while traveling, up to $30 USD per day.
- If you stay with friends or family instead of a hotel, you may expense a host gift of up to
  $25.
