title: "Engineering: offsites and conference travel"
---

How engineering teams plan offsites and travel to conferences. Budgets come from your
department; what you may expense is set by the
[Global Travel and Expense Policy](/handbook/finance/expenses/).

## Planning an offsite

1. Open an offsite issue with the dates, the city and the expected headcount at least six
   weeks ahead, and tag your department's finance business partner.
2. Book the venue and any group activity through Zip if it is over $5,000.
3. Everyone books their own travel in Navan once the issue is approved.

## Getting there

- Flights and trains are booked in Navan. Train rides are standard class with a 7 day booking
  lead time.
- Rental cars are reimbursable up to the compact car class. Share a car where you can.
- Taxis and ride-hailing: up to $300 per trip.
- If you stay with a colleague or friend instead of the offsite hotel, tell the organiser so
  the room block is not charged for you.

## While you are there

- Dry cleaning is allowable if the stay is over 5 days.
- Team dinners are paid by the most senior team member present and submitted by them.

## If something goes wrong

Business travel is covered by the company's business travel accident policy with Chubb. For
lost luggage, the baggage protection is $500 per bag, up to 5 bags. File the claim with Chubb
and keep the airline's report; see [Travel](/handbook/finance/travel/) for the claim contacts.
