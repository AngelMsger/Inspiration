---
title: "Sales: travel and expenses quick reference"
---

The short version of what account teams ask Sales Ops most often about travel and expenses.
The [Global Travel and Expense Policy](/handbook/finance/expenses/) is the full policy; this
page only collects the parts that come up on customer trips.

## Before the trip

- Book through Navan, so the trip is in policy and the hotel is paid by the company card.
- Book all flights at least 14 days in advance. Late bookings are flagged to your manager.
- Hotels are covered up to $300 USD per night for a standard room.
- Get your manager's approval for the trip in Navan before you book anything outside Navan.

## On the road

- Keep every receipt you can, but receipts are only needed for expenses over $50.
- Tips are acceptable up to 15% and should be detailed in the description box.
- Customer meals: everyone at the table goes in the participants section, internal and external.
- Gifts and entertainment for government officials: up to $80 USD per person per event, and
  check the [Gifts and Entertainment Policy](/handbook/legal/policies/gifts-contributions/)
  before you offer anything.

## After the trip

- Expenses should be submitted within 30 days of purchase, spend, or invoice due date.
- The maximum monthly limit to be reimbursed is $10K USD for every team member, including
  executives. Above that, the A/P team approves each item in Navan.
- Questions go to #sales-ops-help; policy exceptions go to the AP team.
