---
title: "Travel booking guidelines"
---

The Travel team's guidelines for booking business travel in Navan. They cover how to book;
what is reimbursable is in the [Global Travel and Expense Policy](/handbook/finance/expenses/).

## Flights

- Search in Navan and pick the lowest logical fare: the cheapest flight that fits the trip
  without an overnight layover or more than one extra connection.
- Choose refundable fares only when the trip is likely to change.
- Unused tickets stay as credit in Navan; use them on your next booking with the same airline.

## Rental cars

- Rental cars must be booked in Navan at least 14 days before pick-up.
- Decline the rental company's insurance in the countries where the company's policy covers
  it; Navan shows the advice for each country.
- Return the car with a full tank: refuelling charges from the rental company are not
  reimbursed.

## Hotels

- Book in Navan so the room is paid with the company's virtual card.
- For conferences, use the event's room block when it is cheaper than Navan.
