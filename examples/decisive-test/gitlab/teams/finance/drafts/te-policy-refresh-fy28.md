**Status: draft for discussion, not approved.** This page collects the changes the AP team
proposes for the FY28 review of the [Global Travel and Expense Policy](/handbook/finance/expenses/).
Until the CFO approves them and the policy page is updated, the current policy applies.

## Proposed changes

### Hotels

- Hotel stays are reimbursable up to $350 USD (or equivalent local currency) inclusive of tax
  per night for standard rooms, with a 7 day booking lead time.
- Reason: Navan's dynamic policy has approved rooms above $300 in most large cities since
  the spring.

### Co-working space

- Daily office passes: $75.00 limit per day.
- The space should be located within a commutable distance (+-80km) of the team member's
  primary residence.
- Reason: day passes are mostly used near home; a lower daily limit and a wider radius match
  how the space is used.

## Open questions

- Do we need a separate limit for conference hotel blocks?
