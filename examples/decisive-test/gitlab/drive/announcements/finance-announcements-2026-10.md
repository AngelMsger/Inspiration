# #finance-announcements, October 2026

Exported from the #finance-announcements channel on 2026-10-31 for the AP team's records.
Posts are in the order they were posted.

---

**2026-10-01, AP team:** From today, Navan auto-approves an out-of-policy flight booking after
48 hours without a manual action from the approver, instead of 24 hours. Approvers now get a
reminder after 24 hours. The policy page will be updated in the next policy release.

---

**2026-10-05, AP team:** Frequent international travellers: from 2026-10-05 you qualify for
business class on flights of 8 hours or more once you take 2 or more such trips per quarter
(it was 3). Everything else about the rule stays the same. The handbook page will follow.

---

**2026-10-12, AP team:** Co-working: from today, more than 3 day-pass days in a month needs
manager and Finance approval (it was more than 5). Monthly memberships are unchanged.

---

**2026-10-15, Travel team:** Domestic flights now need to be booked at least 10 days in
advance, down from 14, effective today. Navan's out-of-policy flag is updated; international
flights stay at 21 days.

---

**2026-10-20, AP team:** Flight class upgrades: from today, the next class up may be booked on
flights over 6 hours of consecutive flight time (each segment 6+ hours), up from 5. Bookings
made before today keep the old rule.

---

**2026-10-27, Travel team:** Hotels must now be booked with at least a 10 day booking lead
time (was 7 days), effective 2026-11-01, so that we get the corporate rates. The policy page
will be updated with the next policy release.
