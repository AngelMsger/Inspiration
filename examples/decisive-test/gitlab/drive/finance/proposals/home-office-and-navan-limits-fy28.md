# Proposal: home office stipend and Navan limits for FY28

Prepared by the AP team for the FY28 budget review, 2026-10-29. For discussion with FP&A; none
of this is approved.

## 1. New hire home office stipend

Proposal: new hires will receive a 2,000 USD (or equivalent local currency) stipend to help
them set up their home office in their first year, up from 1,500 USD.

- Desk and chair prices on the approved list rose about 25% since 2023.
- The yearly refresh for existing team members stays at 500 USD.

## 2. Single-item limit in Navan

Proposal: expense claims for a single item up to $7,500 can be expensed via Navan; above that,
the procurement process in Zip.

- Most items between $5,000 and $7,500 are conference sponsorship add-ons that Zip slows down.

## Next steps

FP&A to model the cost by 2026-11-15; CFO decision in the FY28 planning cycle.
