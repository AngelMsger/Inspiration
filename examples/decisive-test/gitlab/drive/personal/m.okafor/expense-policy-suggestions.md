# Expense policy: my suggested changes

Notes for the FY28 policy review issue. I copied the sections I use most from the policy and
changed the limits to what I think they should be. Not sent yet.

## Books (Audio/E-Books included)

- Limit is set to $100/year.
- Books must be work related; fiction does not count.

Why: a single technical book is often $60 on its own now.

## Postage/Shipping/Customs Fees

- Limit is set at $150.00 per transaction before being flagged for approval.

Why: shipping a monitor back to IT costs more than $100 in most of EMEA.

## Office Supplies & consumables

- No change; $50 a month is enough.
