# Engineering handbook

## On call

Engineers on call carry the pager for one week at a time. On-call engineers who work after midnight can take a taxi home, and the company pays up to 45 USD a day.

## Travel

Meals on business trips are reimbursed up to 50 USD per day.

## Reviews

Every change is reviewed by another engineer before it ships.
