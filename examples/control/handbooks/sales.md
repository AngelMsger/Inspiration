# Sales handbook

## Client visits

Sales staff book client visits a week ahead and log them in the CRM. Sales staff visiting clients may take a taxi, up to 45 USD per day.

## Travel

Meals on business trips are reimbursed up to 50 USD per day.

## Targets

Quarterly targets are set with the regional lead in the first week of each quarter.
