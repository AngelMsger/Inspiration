# Support handbook

## Shifts

Support agents work in two shifts and hand over open tickets at the end of each shift.

## Travel

Meals on business trips are reimbursed up to 50 USD per day.

## Escalation

Agents escalate a ticket to the duty manager when a customer asks twice.
