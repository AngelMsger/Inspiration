# Customer service policy

## Refunds

Refunds are paid within 14 days of approval; customers may object to a decision within 30 days.

## Returns

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery, and damaged items must be reported within 7 days.

## Shipping

Standard orders ship within 3 days; express orders within 1 day.

## Contact

Customers reach the service desk by email or phone on working days.
