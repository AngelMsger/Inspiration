# Travel policy

## Scope

This policy applies to every employee who travels for work. Trips need their manager's approval before booking.

## Lodging

The hotel cap is 200 USD per night. Rooms above the cap need the finance director's approval.

## Meals

The meal allowance is 60 USD per day. It covers breakfast, lunch and dinner on travel days.

## Expense claims

Travel expenses must be claimed within 30 days of the trip, other expenses within 60 days.
